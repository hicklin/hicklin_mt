## If we have worked together

I would be glad of a few sentences. Two or three is plenty, and something specific is worth
far more than something generous — the thing I actually did, or got wrong, or made easier for
you.

Email it to [resume@hicklin.mt](mailto:resume@hicklin.mt) with how you would like to be
credited: name, role at the time, and company. Tell me if you would rather not be named and I
will leave it off, or leave the whole thing out.

If you would rather do it yourself, the site is a
[public repository](https://github.com/hicklin/hicklin_mt) and a pull request adding a file to
`content/testimonials/` is welcome. It is not the easier route and nobody should feel obliged
to take it.
