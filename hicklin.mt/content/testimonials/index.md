These are people I have worked with, quoted with their permission. Where a quote refers to
work on this site, I have linked the case study, so you can read about the thing being described.

> Whenever I have reviewed his PRs I am always impressed by the extensive and comprehensive
> tests he writes to complement the code, as well as the clear and thorough documentation he
> includes. A recent example of his thoroughness is in his contributions to the open-source
> heapless crate. Will was adding some missing features to one of the types provided by the
> crate. He delved into the standard library to see how the feature had been implemented on a
> similar type there, but rather than just take it at face value he made sure to fully
> understand the implications of the implementation to make sure it was suitable for his
> use-case.

<cite>Pete Kubiak — Lead Embedded Engineer, Dyson</cite>

That contribution is [heapless #591](https://github.com/rust-embedded/heapless/pull/591); the
review thread shows the part he is describing.

> I was impressed by his documentation describing the state machine used in the robotic vacuum

<cite>Chris Mapp — Associate Principal Embedded Engineer, Dyson</cite>

That is [the robotic vacuum device type](@/projects/matter-rvc-device-type/index.md).

## If we have worked together

I would be glad of a few sentences. Two or three is plenty, and something specific is worth
far more than something generous — the thing I actually did, or got wrong, or made easier for
you.

Email it to [resume@hicklin.mt](mailto:resume@hicklin.mt) with how you would like to be
credited: name, role at the time, and company. Tell me if you would rather not be named and I
will leave it off, or leave the whole thing out.

If you would rather do it yourself, the site is a
[public repository](https://github.com/hicklin/hicklin_mt) and a pull request adding your words
to [this page](https://github.com/hicklin/hicklin_mt/blob/main/hicklin.mt/content/testimonials/index.md)
is welcome. It is not the easier route and nobody should feel obliged
to take it.
